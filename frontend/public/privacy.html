Privacy Policy — TimeFrame

Last updated: September 9, 2026

TimeFrame ("the app") helps you and your group find shared free time by combining your Google Calendar availability with times you enter manually. This page explains what data the app accesses, what it stores, and how it's used.

What we access from Google Calendar

When you connect Google Calendar, TimeFrame requests read-only access (calendar.readonly) to your primary calendar. We only read:

  • The start and end time of each event
  • The timezone the event was created in

We do not read or store event titles, descriptions, locations, attendees, or any other event details — only the time ranges needed to know when you're busy.

What we store

  • Your Google account email, used as your account identifier
  • Busy time ranges imported from Google Calendar (as described above)
  • Busy time ranges you enter manually through the app
  • Group membership, if you create or join a shared group link

This data is stored on our server for as long as your account is active, or until you disconnect Google Calendar or clear your schedule (see "Your choices" below).

Group sharing

If you join or create a group (via a shared invite link), your busy time ranges — both imported and manually entered — become visible to other members of that same group, so the app can compute a time that works for everyone. Only share a group link with people you're comfortable seeing your availability.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data
  • We don't share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing
  • We don't use your calendar data for anything other than computing free/busy availability within the app

Third-party services we use

These services process data on our behalf to operate the app:

  • Google — for sign-in and reading calendar availability
  • Render — hosts our backend server and database
  • Vercel — hosts the app's web page

Browser storage

The app uses your browser's local storage to remember your session (like your account id and a cached copy of your schedule) so the page works smoothly between visits. This stays on your device and isn't shared with us beyond what's already sent to our server to compute your schedule.

Your choices

  • Click "Logout / Disconnect Google" in the app to remove your imported Google Calendar data from our server
  • Click "Clear Schedule" to remove locally cached data from your browser
  • Email us (below) to request complete deletion of your account data from our server

Children's privacy

TimeFrame is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Continued use of TimeFrame after an update means you accept the revised policy.
